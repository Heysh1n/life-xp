## 🌟 What is LIFE-XP Challenge?

**LIFE-XP Challenge** is a hardcore Minecraft 1.21.1 mod built on a modern Architectury monorepo. It completely overhauls your progression by linking 15 player attributes directly to your Experience Level. 

The more XP you farm, the stronger you become—but dying carries severe, permanent consequences.

---

## ⚙️ Core Features

### 📈 Dynamic Attribute Scaling
Your physical capabilities (Max Health, Attack Damage, Movement Speed, Armor Toughness, and more) scale dynamically as you gain XP. You can configure progression via classic 3-point interpolation or override them with **custom mathematical formulas** (`Base + (Level * Multiplier) ^ Exponent`) for precise modpack balancing.

### ☠️ Brutal Death XP Tax
We completely bypass the forgiving vanilla 100 XP drop cap. Death strips a configurable percentage of your total XP, instantly stripping away your hard-earned power and making every encounter a high-stakes risk.

### 🌫️ Visual Progression (Dynamic Fog)
Experience the world through a dense, blinding fog that physically restricts your vision at level 0 and gradually clears up as you accumulate experience and power.

### 🧪 Custom Utility Items
* **Dynamic XP Bottle:** Channel your XP safely into a storable bottle with a 10% tax, or instantly consume it to regain levels.
* **Life Bottle:** A rare 3-durability epic item that securely saves your entire 41-slot inventory upon death, protecting your gear even from the void.

### 🛠️ Live Server Management
Tweak configs, apply built-in difficulty presets (*Vanilla+*, *BabyMode*, *Life-XP Core*, *Hardcore*), and reload settings live without server restarts via the `/lifexp` command tree (Requires OP Level 2).

## 📦 System Requirements & Dependencies

* **Minecraft Version:** `1.21-1.21.1`
* **Mod Loaders:** `Fabric` or `NeoForge`
* **Required Dependency:** [Yet Another Config Lib (YACL)](https://modrinth.com/mod/yacl) — *Required for the in-game configuration screen.*

---

## 🚀 How to Install

1. Download a modern launcher (**Modrinth App**, or other Launcher).
2. Install **Fabric Loader** or **NeoForge** for Minecraft `1.21.1`.
3. Download the corresponding `.jar` file for your loader from the releases page.
4. Download **Yet Another Config Lib (YACL)**.
5. Drop both `.jar` files into your `mods` folder and launch the game!
